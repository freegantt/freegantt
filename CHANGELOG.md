# Changelog

This file records every change a consumer of FreeGantt can see. The newest release is at the top.

The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). A breaking change does not change the major version before 1.0.

## [Unreleased]

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-02

Dependencies and scheduling. `entryDependencies()` stores links between Entries and draws them as connectors. A user connects two bars by drag, and selects, labels and deletes a line. `scheduling()` keeps the links true: a move pushes the successors, in working days when you pass a working calendar. It also reads constraints and manually scheduled Entries, and it writes float, critical and a driving path. Both plugins use generic seams that any plugin can use. The seams are connector providers, the connect gesture, plugin store writes with an edit, and an extender that sees store-only changes. Read the new guide [Dependencies and scheduling](docs/13-dependencies-and-scheduling.md).

### Added

- A bar label outside its bar draws above the connector lines, with a halo in the pane color, so a line behind it never cuts the text. The new `--fg-bar-label-halo-color` token sets the halo color, and `transparent` turns it off.
- The harness "Dependencies" page shows connector arrowheads and has an Arrowheads box that switches `connectorArrow`.
- The new `connectorArrow: 'end' | 'none'` Gantt option changes live. The default `'end'` draws a filled arrowhead at the arrival end of every connector, pointing into the target bar's edge. `'none'` draws none. The arrowhead stays inside the 12 px stub, so no route changes. Two tokens style it: `--fg-connector-arrow-size` (default `8px`) and `--fg-connector-arrow-color` (default the line colour, so a selected connector gets the selected colour). New type `ConnectorArrow` and Part `.fg-connector-arrow`.
- The new `fitDatasetPaddingPx` Gantt option (also on `TimeScaleModel`) sets the empty axis before the first bar and after the last bar under `range: 'fitDataset'`. It changes live. The default is `24` px, room for a connector stub and its arrowhead, so a hook into the first bar stays in view. `0` removes the margin. A pinned `range` has none. A `'fitDataset'` axis now starts 24 px before the first date and ends 24 px after the last.
- Connector lines now pass behind the bars they cross. The selected line and the label text still draw above the bars. A click on a line between bars still selects it.
- Connecting two bars is easier. A drop anywhere on a bar joins its nearer edge: the left half is `start`, the right half is `end`. A drop on a port still joins that port's edge. While you drag, the bar under the pointer gets an outline, its snap edge shows an active port, and every connectable bar shows its ports. The port hit area is 20 px wide (32 px on a coarse pointer) and as tall as the bar, and the dot is 10 px. The resize handle keeps the middle of the edge. The new `.fg-connect-target` outline class marks the target bar, and `ConnectDraft` gains `targetBarId` and `connectableBarIds`.
- The harness "Dependencies" page installs `scheduling()`. A connect drag onto a bar that starts too early moves that bar, and one undo puts the bar back and removes the line.
- `scheduling()` moves a successor in the same step as a new Dependency. `entryDependencies().add()` and the connect drag push a successor that starts too early, and its followers, with `pushOnly` rules. A manually scheduled Entry stays, and constraints apply. One undo removes the link and the move, and the float follows in the same step. A removed Dependency moves nothing. `replaceAll()` pushes too, in a sync change set with no undo step.
- `EditRequest` gains `changedStoreRows`: the plugin store rows the transaction changes, each with its `from` and `to` row. A transaction that writes only a plugin store already reached the edit extension hook. Now the extender can see which rows changed, read the store as the commit leaves it, and answer with Entry edits in the same ChangeSet, so one undo reverts both. A drag preview and a transaction with no store write give an empty list.
- `ctx.view.refreshConnectors()` asks every connector provider again and redraws the connectors. A plugin calls it when screen-only state changes, such as a selected connector. Several calls before the next frame give one redraw. It writes nothing to the Dataset, makes no undo step and fires no change event.
- The harness "Dependencies" page has a backward finish-to-start link, `dep-back`, that shows the route along the gap above its target row.
- The new `connectorRouting: { avoidBars, maxAvoidRows }` Gantt option changes live. A vertical connector segment now slides sideways to miss bars in the rows it crosses, up to 48 px from its default place. `avoidBars` defaults to `true`. A vertical across more than `maxAvoidRows` rows (default `200`) keeps its default place. New type `ConnectorRouting`.
- Connector routes read better. A backward finish-to-start runs along the near gap of the target row, not the source row. Start-to-start and finish-to-finish draw one vertical outside both bars, with two bends. A same-row backward connector loops along the bottom gap of its row. The stub is 12 px, not 8.
- The harness "Scheduling" page has a constraint form. It sets "start no earlier than", "finish no later than" or "must start on" on a chosen task, and it clears the constraint. A list names the task that breaks a ceiling, or keeps a must-start-on date and breaks a Dependency.
- `scheduling().setConstraint(entry, constraint)` sets a `startNoEarlierThan`, `finishNoLaterThan` or `mustStartOn` constraint on one Entry. It moves the Entry and its successors to meet the constraint, in one undo step, and `diagnostics()` names a constraint the Entry breaks. A user drag keeps a `mustStartOn` or `finishNoLaterThan` constraint and replaces only a `startNoEarlierThan` floor. `EntryConstraint` now holds all three kinds.
- `schedule()` reads two more constraint kinds. `{ kind: 'finishNoLaterThan', date }` is a ceiling: the engine never moves an Entry to meet it. When a dependency pushes the finish past it, the dependency wins and the new `constraint-violated` diagnostic names the constraint and the Entry. `{ kind: 'mustStartOn', date }` is a hard date. It wins over a dependency, and a dependency it breaks gives a `blocked-move` diagnostic with the new reason `'mustStartOn'`. The order is: manually scheduled, `mustStartOn`, dependency, `startNoEarlierThan`. `defaultPolicy.precedence` and `ScheduleRuleKind` gain `'mustStartOn'`. New types `ScheduleConstraintKind` and `ConstraintViolationDiagnostic`.
- `scheduling()` declares two Fields, `float` (days) and `critical` (boolean), and writes them with every edit that moves a date, in the same change set, so one undo restores them. A Dataset as built, a replaced or added Dependency, and an undo refresh them in one sync change set with no undo step. `float` is the days a leaf Entry can start later without moving the project finish. It counts workdays when `scheduling({ calendar })` has a calendar. `critical` is true when `float` is zero or less. A summary Entry, an Entry with a missing date and a loop member have neither. Core paints nothing: style critical bars with a variant rule, `{ name: 'critical', when: { critical: true } }`. Both Fields take `editable: 'api'`, so a grid column can show them. Constraints do not change float.
- `scheduling().drivingPath(entryId)` returns the Entry ids, in order, of the chain of predecessors that holds an Entry in place. The Entry comes last. A loop member gives `[]`.
- `calculateFloat({ entries, dependencies, calendar? })` and `findDrivingPath(request, entryId)` are new pure exports. They need no plugin. New types `FloatRequest`, `FloatResult` and `EntryFloat`.
- The harness has a "Critical path" page. A variant rule paints the critical bars, and a "Show driving path" button prints the chain behind the selected task.
- `schedule({ calendar })` counts workdays. `calendar` is `{ time, calendar?, ofEntry? }`: the Dataset's `ZonedTime`, a `WorkingCalendar` for every Entry, and a map from Entry id to its own `WorkingCalendar`. A day lag skips the days off, a negative lag too. A moved Entry keeps its length in workdays. A lag counts in the calendar of its successor, the default of Primavera P6. A week, month or year lag gives `unsupported-lag-unit` when a calendar applies. With no calendar, a day is 24 hours as before. New type `ScheduleCalendar`.
- `scheduling({ calendar, calendars, calendarField })` counts lags and moves in workdays. Pass the same `WorkingCalendar` that `timeShading()` shades from, and the shading and the schedule agree. `calendars` is a record of named calendars. `calendarField` is the key of a Field whose value names one. An Entry with no value, or an unknown name, uses `calendar`, then 24-hour days. All three are optional.
- The harness "Dependencies" page selects a connector by click or Tab, moves the selection with the arrow keys, and removes it with Delete. One undo restores it. A readout names the Dependency that a key removed. axe checks the page.
- `ConnectorInput` gains `showLabel?: boolean`. With `showLabel: true` and a `label`, core draws the label as SVG text, class `fg-connector-text`, at the middle of the line. The text is `aria-hidden`, because the line keeps the label as its accessible name. No `showLabel`, no text node. `entryDependencies({ showLabels: true })` turns it on for every Dependency connector. Default `false`.
- `entryDependencies()` owns connector selection. A click or focus on a connector selects its Dependency and sets the `selected` flag. A click or focus elsewhere clears it. The Entry Selection does not change. A connector is focusable: arrows move focus to the next or previous connector, and `Delete` removes the focused Dependency, with one undo step. On any other target the keys keep their core meaning. `entryDependencies().on('beforeDependencyRemove', handler)` may veto: return `false`, or a Promise of `false`. `on('dependencyRemove', handler)` fires after the remove. Both fire for the keyboard remove only, not for `remove(id)`. `on` returns the call that removes the handler. New types `DependencyEvents`, `DependencyRemoval`, `BeforeDependencyRemoveHandler` and `DependencyRemoveHandler`.
- `entryDependencies({ labelField })` names each Entry in the connector label "<from> → <to>" from that Field. Default `name`. The label is always the connector's accessible name.
- `ctx.view.dom.connectorFor(id)` returns the mounted connector line for a provider id, or `undefined` when the frame draws none. A plugin moves focus to it, like `barFor` for a bar.
- A connector line takes the pointer on its stroke only, and a connector with a `label` takes focus. One labelled connector is the tab stop: the one with the `selected` flag, else the first in frame order. The others have `tabindex="-1"`. `CONNECTOR_FLAG_KEYS` gains `'selected'`. It sets `data-flag="selected"` and `aria-current="true"`, and `.fg-connector[data-flag~="selected"]` is the style hook.
- A `ctx.interaction.registerKeyHandler` handler may return `false` to decline a key. The keymap then offers the key to the next older binding, and core keeps its own meaning. Before, any matching handler took the key and called `preventDefault`. New type `KeyHandler`. A plugin that binds `Delete` or an arrow must decline every target it does not own.
- `ctx.view.dom.targetUnder` and `ctx.view.onDomEvent` now resolve a drawn connector line. `TargetKind` gains `'connector'`, and `DomTarget` gains `connectorId`, the id the provider gave. A connector stands for no Entry, so `entryIds` is empty.
- A decoration provider can return `{ kind: 'rowSpan', rowId, start, end, class? }`. The Gantt paints a box inside that row between the two Instants, on `underBars` or `overBars`. The node has the class `fg-row-span` and the consumer's `class`, and it takes no pointer events and no focus. A row outside the window paints nothing. The new type `RowSpan` is the resolved shape. The optional `text` is a plain string the Gantt paints at the span's end, inside the same node.
- The harness has a "Planned vs actual" page. A plugin reads two consumer Fields and draws a row span of the planned dates under each actual bar. It writes the slip as the span's `text`, for example "+4d", on each late Entry.
- New `workingCalendar({ workdays, holidays })` returns an immutable `WorkingCalendar`. It answers `isWorkday(day, time)`, `addWorkdays(instant, count, time)` and `workdaysBetween(a, b, time)` in the Dataset zone, and `calendar.offTime()` returns a cover that `timeShading([{ covers: calendar.offTime() }])` shades. The library ships no holiday list. New types `WorkingCalendarOptions`, `OffTimeCover` and `Workday`, and `InvalidWorkingCalendarError` for a bad weekday or holiday. The generic harness page shades its weekends and two holidays from one calendar.
- `ctx.view.registerConnector(provider)` draws a line between two bars. The provider returns `ConnectorInput` values: an `id`, a `from` and a `to` end (`{ entryId, edge: 'start' | 'end' }`), and optional `flags`, `class` and `label`. Core finds both bars for any Entry, routes an orthogonal path and clips it to the window, so a line stays drawn while one end is off screen. An end inside a collapsed group attaches to its nearest visible ancestor. `GeometryFrame.connectors` holds the lines, and the timeline pane draws them as SVG paths with `data-connector-id`, `data-flag` and an accessible name from `label`. `CONNECTOR_FLAG_KEYS` and the types `ConnectorInput`, `ConnectorEnd`, `ConnectorEdge`, `ConnectorFlags` and `ConnectorProvider` are public. The harness has a new "Related to" page.
- `schedule()` and `defaultPolicy` are new exports. `schedule({ entries, dependencies, proposed, manuallyScheduled })` is a pure function. It moves each successor to the earliest date that meets each `FS`, `SS`, `FF` and `SF` dependency, with a positive or negative lag in fixed-length units, and answers `{ patch, diagnostics }`. A cycle names its exact member Entries. A manually scheduled Entry never moves, and the engine reports the move it skipped. It never writes a date you proposed. New types `ScheduleRequest`, `ScheduleResult`, `ScheduleChange`, `ScheduleDiagnostic`, `ScheduleDependency` and `SchedulingPolicy`. It needs no plugin.
- `schedule()` follows its predecessors in both directions by default: a successor moves earlier too. Pass `successorMoves: 'pushOnly'` to move successors later only. Pass `constraints`, a map from Entry id to `{ kind: 'startNoEarlierThan', date }`, to stop a start from moving before a date. A dependency still moves the Entry later than that date. A manually scheduled Entry keeps its dates, and the `blocked-move` diagnostic names the broken dependency. New types `ScheduleConstraint` and `SuccessorMoves`.
- `ctx.edits.writeRowsWithEdit(writer)` lets a data plugin change rows of its own store with each edit and removal. The writer receives the `EditRequest` and answers `StoreRowWrite` values, `{ id, row }`, where `row: undefined` removes the row. The edit and the row writes land in one change set, and one undo restores both. A throw in the writer aborts the transaction. A store write inside the writer still throws `MutationDuringExtensionHookError`. New types `EditRowWriter` and `StoreRowWrite`.
- `overridingProposed(fields, edit)` lets a plugin's edit result replace a Field the user proposed on the same Entry. The marked value wins in the same change set, and one undo restores the previous value. An unmarked write to a proposed Field still throws in dev mode.
- `PluginStore.syncAll(rows)` makes a plugin's store hold exactly the rows in a `Map`. It writes, adds and removes rows in one change set with origin `'sync'`, as `entries.syncAll` does: no undo step, and Redo stays. An id with no Entry throws `EntryNotFoundError` and writes nothing.
- New `entryDependencies()` plugin stores Dependencies between Entries: `{ id, fromId, toId, type: 'FS' | 'SS' | 'FF' | 'SF', lag }`, with the lag in days and a negative lag allowed. Install it with `new Dataset({ plugins: [dependencies] })`. Then call `dependencies.replaceAll(list)`, `add(dependency)`, `remove(id)`, `all()` and `predecessorsOf(entry)`. `add` and `remove` are undoable. `replaceAll` records no undo step and throws, with no write, for an unknown Entry id. A lag in another unit throws `UnsupportedLagUnitError`, and a repeated id throws `DuplicateDependencyIdError`. Removing an Entry removes every Dependency that names it, in the same change set. The Gantt draws one connector for each Dependency, named "<from name> → <to name>" for a screen reader. It needs no scheduling plugin. New exports: `entryDependencies`, `ENTRY_DEPENDENCIES_ID`, the types `Dependency`, `DependencyInput`, `DependencyLag`, `DependencyType` and `EntryDependencies`, and `DependencyStoreNotInstalledError`.
- The harness has a "Dependencies" page. It installs `entryDependencies()` on a fixture with all four types and one negative lag. A "Replace dependencies" button swaps the whole set, and scroll, zoom and collapsed groups stay as they are.
- A connect drag now adds a Dependency. `entryDependencies()` registers the connect handler, so a bar shows a port at each edge. The pair of edges sets the type: end→start `FS`, start→start `SS`, end→end `FF`, start→end `SF`. The lag is 0 days, and one undo step removes the Dependency. A drop on the same Entry, or a repeat of a stored Dependency with the same ends and type, adds nothing. Pass `entryDependencies({ connectByDrag: false })` for an app that only shows Dependencies: no port draws. New type `EntryDependenciesOptions`. The "Dependencies" harness page drags each type.
- `ctx.view.onConnect(handler)` switches on the connect gesture. While a plugin registers a handler, the hovered bar shows a connector port beside each edge. The user drags from a port and drops on a port of another bar, and a preview line follows the pointer. The Gantt fires `beforeConnect`, then calls each handler, then fires `connect`. All three carry `{ from, to }`, two `{ entryId, edge }` ends. The payload never names a dependency type, so the plugin maps the pair to its own record. `beforeConnect` may veto, and its handler may return a Promise: the line stays drawn, dimmed, until the Promise settles, and Escape ends the wait. A drop on the same Entry's own edge, or on no port, does nothing. With no handler, no port draws and no event fires. The new capability `connect` gates the port and the drag together: `capabilities: { connect: (entry) => … }`, and an Entry that cannot connect shows no port. A locked Entry keeps `connect` open unless `capabilities.connect` says no. New types `ConnectEnds` and `ConnectHandler`, and the new report code `connect-handler-failed`. There is no keyboard connect: add a connector from your own form through the host API of your plugin. The "Related to" harness page drags a new connector, shows an async hold and shows a locked Entry with no port.
- New `scheduling()` plugin keeps Dependencies true. Install it after `entryDependencies()`. Moving an Entry moves its successors in the same change set, so one undo restores every Entry. A successor follows its predecessor earlier too. Pass `successorMoves: 'pushOnly'` to move successors later only.
- `scheduling()` declares a boolean Field `manuallyScheduled`. Bind a grid column to it for a toggle. The plugin never moves an Entry with the flag on, and a broken Dependency gives a diagnostic.
- A date a user sets on a scheduled Entry becomes its "start no earlier than" constraint. `scheduling().constraintOf(id)` reads it and `clearConstraint(id)` removes it. The Dependency still wins: the Entry lands where the Dependency allows, and the date stays as the floor. Pass `editedDate: 'userDateWins'` to keep the user's date instead. Then a `blocked-move` diagnostic marks the broken link.
- `scheduling().on('scheduleDiagnostics', handler)` reports cycles, blocked moves and unreadable Dependencies after each commit. `scheduling().diagnostics()` reads the latest. The connectors of a cycle carry the `cycle` flag.
- New error `SchedulingNotInstalledError` (`code: 'scheduling-not-installed'`) for a `scheduling()` call before a Dataset installs it.
- The harness has a "Scheduling" page. It installs `scheduling()` on a chain of six tasks joined by all four Dependency types and one negative lag. Drag a task later and its successors move. Drag it earlier and they follow. One undo restores them all. A Manual column toggles `manuallyScheduled`, and a loop of three tasks shows its members from the diagnostics.

### Changed

- A finish-to-start connector to a bar that starts at, or within two stubs (24 px) after, the end of its source now takes one bend. The line runs right from the source, then drops onto the successor's top edge (or rises onto its bottom edge), and the arrowhead points into the bar. A start-to-finish connector does the same, mirrored. It used to zig-zag back across both bars. `ConnectorAnchor` gains `barTop` and `barBottom`. A bar in the way of the new vertical keeps the old route.
- A `startNoEarlierThan` floor now moves an Entry that has no incoming dependency. A floor later than the start moves the Entry later. Before, such a floor changed nothing.

### Fixed

- The grid header no longer draws a divider through the column names when the timeline header has two bands. The grid header draws one divider at its bottom edge.
- A grab on a connector port now always starts a connect drag. The port sat on top of the bar's resize handle, so a grab on the dot started a resize. The port now sits outside the bar, past the handle, and the new `--fg-connector-port-gap` token (default `4px`) sets that space.
- `entries.syncAll()` drops the collapse state of a row the new list removes. `gantt.collapsed` no longer lists an id that names no row, and `collapseChange` announces the drop. A kept row stays collapsed, and scroll position and zoom stay as they were. `entries.load()` is unchanged: it starts the view over by design.
- Connectors follow a drag. While you move or resize a bar, each line ends on the preview place of the bar, and on the preview place of each successor the drag pushes. On release the lines no longer jump, and Escape sends them back.
- Resize handles and connect ports follow a drag. While you move or resize a bar, its two handles and its two ports sit on the preview edges of the bar. Before, they stayed at the committed place. On release or Escape they go back.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-30

The first release. Install it with `npm install freegantt`.

### Changed

- The four keyboard steps (`freegantt.moveEntryUp`, `freegantt.moveEntryDown`, `freegantt.indentEntry`, `freegantt.outdentEntry`) act on every selected Entry, from the keys and from the context menu, in both panes. Each Entry takes the step it would take alone and asks its own `reorder` capability, lock rule and place rule. A refused Entry stays, and the others still move. `up` and `indent` go from the top row down, and `down` and `outdent` go from the bottom row up. Each moved Entry fires its own `beforeEntryMove` and `entryMove`, and one key press is one undo step. One `entry-step-refused` report names every refused Entry. A `beforeEntryMove` handler may answer with a Promise. The Gantt waits for each answer in turn, then writes every accepted move at once. ([#639](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/639))
- The generic demo page starts its data 2 weeks before today. Its Mobilization date line sits 2 days after today and reads "can't move before this". The editing-and-data page uses the same label. ([#627](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/627))
- **Breaking:** `Delete` on a bar removes the Entry the bar draws. It no longer clears the dates. A parent's bar removes the parent and its subtree, and the remove rule can refuse. The command `freegantt.clearDates` clears dates. ([#648](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/648))
- The report `entry-remove-refused` now says "Entries", not "rows": "A remove rule refuses these Entries, or an Entry below them: …". ([#648](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/648))
- `Alt+ArrowUp` and `Alt+ArrowDown` on the timeline pane no longer move focus to the bar in the next row. They move the Entry in the tree. `ArrowUp` and `ArrowDown` still move focus. ([#614](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/614))
- An edit extender and a removal extender read their own Fields typed. `EditRequest`, `EditExtender`, `RemovalExtender`, their wrappers and `DatasetEditHook` take a `TProps` parameter, and `ctx.edits` carries the plugin's own props. `moveEntryTo` accepts any `StoredEntry<TProps>`. ([#626](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/626))
- `parentId` is now `editable: 'anywhere'`. A vertical drag may re-parent an Entry; the grid cell still shows no editor for it, because `entryId` ships no `parseValue`. ([#425](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/425))
- **Breaking:** `FieldLockRule` always returns a `FieldEditable` answer; it no longer returns `undefined` for "no opinion". Call `next(query, field)` in place of returning `undefined` to defer to the next rule. ([#612](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/612))

### Added

- The editing-and-data harness page shows `bufferTrailsTask()`, a plugin over `ctx.edits.setExtender` and `ctx.edits.setRemovalExtender`. A buffer follows the `end` of its task and leaves with it, in one undo step. ([#620](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/620))
- A locked Entry looks locked. The library sets `data-locked` on its row and on each of its bars, and clears it when the Entry unlocks. A plugin that changes a bar move rule with no write calls `ctx.edits.rulesChanged()`, and the next frame repaints the attribute. The built-in themes show `cursor: not-allowed` on a locked bar. A refused drop shows its reason in a `.fg-drop-note` beside the pointer. On release, the Gantt raises the new `entry-drop-refused` report, and the live region announces it. ([#649](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/649))
- The `editing-and-data` and generic harness pages lock an Entry with a padlock toggle column. It replaces the lock checkbox. On `editing-and-data` it asks before a parent unlocks. ([#641](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/641))
- A grid column can be a toggle: `{ field: 'done', toggle: true }` on a `boolean` Field. One click, or `Space` or `Enter` on the focused cell, writes the opposite value as one undo step, and the cell opens no editor. `toggle: { on, off }` sets an icon for each value, and `onToggle` replaces the default write (call its `announceEdit()` after your write so `entryEdit` fires). The Field's `editable`, the lock rule, `capabilities.edit` and `beforeChange` gate it, and a closed toggle draws its value, does nothing, and carries `aria-readonly="true"`. The library writes the accessible name (`role="checkbox"`, `aria-checked`, the column `header`). New types `ColumnToggle` and `ColumnToggleContext`, new command `freegantt.switchToggle`, and `ToggleFieldNotBooleanError` for a toggle on a Field that is not a boolean. ([#641](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/641), [#646](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/646))
- A grid column takes its own `headerRenderer`, beside `columnRenderer`. It wins over the Gantt-wide `headerRenderer` for its column, and the library adds the `header` string as visually hidden text beside the output, so a header that draws only an icon or an inline SVG (`{ html: '<svg …>' }`) keeps its accessible name. New types `ColumnHeaderRenderer` and `ColumnHeaderRendererContext`. ([#641](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/641))
- A keybinding chord names the space bar as `Space`, for example `Shift+Space`. ([#641](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/641))
- New command `freegantt.clearDates`, labelled "Clear dates". It clears `start` and `end` of the selected Entries and keeps them. It has no key, and the context menu lists it only when an Entry has dates of its own to clear. A refusal by a lock or `beforeChange` writes nothing and raises one `info` report, code `entry-clear-dates-refused`. ([#648](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/648))
- The keyboard moves an Entry in the tree. In the grid pane, with a row or a cell focused, `Alt+ArrowUp` and `Alt+ArrowDown` move it one sibling up or down. `Alt+Shift+ArrowRight` makes it the last child of the sibling above it. `Alt+Shift+ArrowLeft` moves it just after its parent, and its later siblings stay with the parent. In the timeline pane, with a bar focused, `Alt+ArrowUp` and `Alt+ArrowDown` move the row. The horizontal arrows on a bar keep their time meaning, so a bar reaches indent and outdent through the context menu. Each chord runs a new command: `freegantt.moveEntryUp`, `freegantt.moveEntryDown`, `freegantt.indentEntry`, `freegantt.outdentEntry`. A context menu lists them too, so a user with no drag has a path. Each step asks the same `reorder` capability, lock rule and place rule as a drag, fires `beforeEntryMove` and `entryMove` with `shiftsTime: false`, and is one undo step. A refused step changes nothing and raises the new `entry-step-refused` report, which the live region announces. ([#614](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/614))
- A row drag that rests in the "into" zone of a collapsed parent for 700 ms expands it, so the drop can land between its children. It applies to a vertical bar drag and to a grid row drag. The expand is view state: it writes no Dataset change and adds no undo step. A cancelled drag leaves the parent expanded. ([#604](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/604))
- A row drag scrolls the rows when the pointer rests within 40 px of the top or bottom edge of the rows viewport. The speed grows toward the edge, up to 900 px per second. It applies to a vertical bar drag and to a grid row drag. Escape, a drop, and the end of the content stop it. ([#603](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/603))
- A Dataset plugin can remove an Entry: `ctx.edits.setRemovalExtender(wrap)` takes a `RemovalExtender`, `(request) => ReadonlySet<EntryId>`. The removal lands in the same `ChangeSet` and the same undo step as the edit that caused it. New types `RemovalExtender` and `RemovalExtenderWrapper`. ([#629](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/629))
- A vertical drag on a bar moves its Entry to another row: before a row, after a row, or into a row as a child. The drag picks a time axis or a row axis after 4 px of travel and holds it for the whole gesture. A tree row splits into three drop zones, an insertion line marks where the bar lands, a refused drop paints `data-drop="refused"` with a `not-allowed` cursor and commits nothing, and one drop is one transaction that one undo reverses. ([#425](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/425))
- New capability `reorder`. `gantt.setCapabilityRule('reorder', false)` turns off vertical drag for every row. ([#425](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/425))
- `entryMove` and `beforeEntryMove` carry `place` and `currentPlace` (a new `TreePlace` type) when a drag changes the tree, and `shiftsTime` when a drag changes the dates. A handler tells a vertical move from a horizontal one. **Breaking:** `EntryMove` is now a discriminated union on `shiftsTime` — `start`/`end` are `Instant` only when `shiftsTime` is `true`; a handler narrows on it before reading either. This lets a tree-only move report an undated Entry, for #602's grid row drag. ([#425](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/425), [#602](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/602))
- New report code `rollup-preview-failed`: a plugin aggregator threw while a drag previewed the new parent's Rollup dates. The frame paints with no Rollup ghost, and the drag goes on. ([#425](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/425))
- The hierarchy-and-timeline harness page's phase-plugin tree is now opt-in (`?tree=phase`); by default the page drags like the generic page. The generic harness page gains a "Lock tree" checkbox. ([#425](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/425))
- A drag on a grid row moves its Entry before, after or into another row, with the same drop zones, Insertion line and refusal as a vertical bar drag. `reorder: false` turns off both. ([#602](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/602))
- `locked` is a new core `Entry` Field. A locked Entry protects only its own row: its cells, its bar and its own delete refuse the user at preview. Its children still move, resize, reorder, leave and join, and a user may drop an Entry under a locked parent. A locked parent's summary bar does not move, and the Rollup still writes its dates. App code still writes past the lock; only the user-facing doors close. Set it through `entries.update(id, { locked: true })`. List `'locked'` in `gridColumns` to give the user a lock toggle. The Field brings a default column (header "Locked", centered, 80 px wide) and a checkbox editor. The default grid shows no lock column. `capabilities.edit` closes the toggle. ([#612](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/612), [#638](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/638))
- New seam `ctx.edits.setPlaceRule(wrap)` on `DatasetEditHook`, with the matching `PlaceRule`, `PlaceQuery` and `PlaceRuleWrapper` types. A plugin's place rule refuses a drop into or out of an Entry, for the bar drag and the grid row drag alike, and `entries.add()`/`entries.update()` throw the new `PlaceRefusedError` (thrown code `place-refused`) when app code crosses it. Core installs no place rule. A `PlaceQuery` holds `entry`, `parent` and `currentParent`, each a `FieldLockQuery`, so a rule asks `place.parent?.isDescendantOf(root)` with no `ctx.dataset` lookup. ([#612](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/612))
- New door `ctx.edits.rulesChanged()` on `DatasetEditHook`. Call it after a lock rule or a place rule's answer changes with no dataset write — a rule that reads the clock, for example — so every bound Gantt re-resolves its affordances. ([#612](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/612))
- The generic, editing-and-data, data, and hierarchy harness pages lock a row through the core `locked` Field, not a hand-built plugin. `harness/plugins/lock-entries.ts` is replaced by `freezePastWork()`: a plugin that locks an Entry once its own work is past, and refuses a drop into a parent whose work is all past, through the public `setLockRule`/`setPlaceRule` seams alone. ([#612](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/612))
- New seam `ctx.edits.setRemoveRule(wrap)` on `DatasetEditHook`, with the matching `RemoveRule`, `RemoveQuery` and `RemoveRuleWrapper` types. A plugin's remove rule refuses `entries.remove()`, asked once for the removed id and once for every member of its subtree, and `entries.remove()` throws the new `RemoveRefusedError` (thrown code `remove-refused`) when app code crosses it. The core lock installs one too: a locked row, or a row holding a locked descendant, now opens its removal to the API only. `RemoveQuery.currentParent` is a `FieldLockQuery | undefined`. ([#611](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/611))
- A row Delete on a locked row, or on a parent that holds a locked row, removes nothing. It raises one `info` report with code `entry-remove-refused` that names the refused rows. A Delete on several rows is all or nothing, and it is one undo step. A user may still delete an unlocked child of a locked row. ([#611](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/611))
- New seam `ctx.edits.setBarMoveRule(wrap)` on `DatasetEditHook`, with the matching `BarMoveRule` and `BarMoveRuleWrapper` types. A plugin's bar move rule answers whether the bar of an Entry moves, for the drag and the keyboard nudge alike. A plugin freezes a summary bar with it, or releases the bar of a locked parent. Core installs its lock rule, remove rule and bar move rule before every plugin, so a plugin wraps each one and can narrow or widen it. A plugin that calls `next()` leaves the core lock in force. ([#638](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/638))

## [0.0.1] - 2026-09-28

The first release on npm: `npm install freegantt`.

### Changed

- **Breaking:** The default date editor opens on a date that has a time of day. It no longer refuses it, and the `time-of-day` report code is removed. When the user commits the same day, the stored time of day stays. A new day stores the start of that day. ([#596](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/596))
- **Breaking:** `DateInput` has a new required member, `showsTimeOfDay`. `inlineEditing()` now applies the date-only end rule to each control that reports `false`, not only to the default control. A consumer date picker on `end` opens on the last covered day, and a typed day stores the start of the next day. A control that reports `true` works as before. ([#579](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/579))
- The default `zoomPresets` has ten steps, not nine. `quarterAndYear` sits between `monthAndYear` and `year`, so one zoom-out step no longer jumps from months to years. To keep the old steps, filter it out of `gantt.zoomPresets`. ([#101](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/101))
- `sum`, `min` and `max` also fold `Duration` values in milliseconds, and answer a `Duration`. `docs/05` shows a computed Field that sums its children. ([#582](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/582))
- **Breaking:** `dataset.entries.sync(rows)` is now `dataset.entries.syncAll(rows)`. The behavior does not change. The new name pairs with `syncChanges`: `syncAll` takes every row, and `syncChanges` takes only the rows that changed. ([#527](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/527))
- `dataset.entries.add()`, `load()`, `syncAll()`, `EntryDelta.upsert`, and the `Dataset` constructor's `entries` option now also take a plain `EntryInput<TProps>` row, not only `FlatEntryInput<TProps>`. A function generic over `TProps` can now pass an `EntryInput<TProps>` value straight through, with no cast. ([#527](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/527))
- The ingest warning for a flat key that no Field declares now says that the value drops. It also names both fixes: declare the key in `fields`, or nest it as `props: { key }`. ([#524](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/524))
- **Breaking:** `formatDate` is now a Formatter: `formatDate(value, ctx)`, date only, `''` for a missing value. It no longer takes an `options` parameter. `ctx` is a `FormatContext` (`{ timeZone, locale }`), where `locale` may be `undefined` as a value. Migrate `formatDate(v, ctx, opts)` to `dateFormatter(opts)(v, ctx)`. ([#577](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/577))
- **Breaking:** `HeaderFormat` now takes one `ctx: FormatContext` in place of positional `zone` and `locale`: `(value, ctx) => string`. `formatHour` and `formatWeekNumber` follow, and both give `''` for a missing value. ([#577](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/577))
- **Breaking:** The `dateOnlyEnd` Dataset option and the `DateOnlyEndRule` type are removed. A date-only `end` string always means "through that day" now: it stores the start of the next day. Pass a timed string, such as `'2026-09-09T00:00:00'`, for an exclusive end. ([#577](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/577))
- **Breaking:** `formatEndInclusive` is removed. Use `formatInclusiveDate` as a Field's `formatValue`, or call `lastCoveredInstant` and pass its result to `formatDate` directly. ([#577](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/577))
- The core `end` Field now names the shipped `formatInclusiveDate` as its `formatValue`, in place of a private, unexported formatter that did the same job. ([#577](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/577))
- `formatValue` is now a consumer override every core Field accepts, not only `start` and `end`. ([#577](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/577))
- **Breaking:** `measureDuration` and `DurationMeasure` are removed. A parent's duration is the span of its rolled-up start and end. The gaps between children count. ([#428](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/428))
- **Breaking:** `entry.duration()`, `ComputeContext.duration()` and `RollUpContext.durations()` are removed. Read `entry.read('duration')`, `ctx.read('duration')` or `ctx.values('duration')`. ([#428](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/428))
- `duration` is now a normal computed Field. A consumer `formatValue` override works on it as on any other Field. ([#428](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/428))
- A bar's accessible label names a one-day bar's date once, instead of reading it twice as a start and an end. ([#578](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/578))

  `gantt.formatFieldValue` alike. ([#583](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/583))

- `DuplicateEntryIdError`'s `kind` gains `'upsert-and-remove'`: an id named in both `EntryDelta.upsert` and `EntryDelta.remove` throws instead of silently picking a winner. ([#527](https://github.com/freegantt/freegantt/issues/527))
