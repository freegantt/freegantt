## 1. Decisions

### D-S3-14 — Viewport gestures live in `view/`, not `interaction/`

Wheel zoom, wheel pan, and keyboard pan mutate no data. They are `view/` attachments:

- `attachWheelNavigation(pane, ctx)` — ctrl/⌘+wheel → `zoomBy(factor, offsetX)`; shift+wheel → pan; `passive: false` + `preventDefault()`
- `attachKeyboardNavigation(container, ctx)` — Page/Home/End and unselected-arrow pan mode

Exempt from arm-threshold, escape-cancel, and one-transaction-per-gesture invariants (no writes).

`attachKeyboardEditing` stays in `interaction/` (writes data). Shell multiplexes one `keydown` listener for the D-S3-13 mode switch.

Closes D-S1.12-17 deferred viewport gestures (`[S3-A7]`).
