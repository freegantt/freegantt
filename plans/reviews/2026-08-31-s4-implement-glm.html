Top recommendation

Candidate 1 — one module reads the tree as of this commit, then candidate 2 — proposed fields in the edit interface.

Candidate 1 is the smallest change with the widest reach: it deletes two copies, fixes the quadratic promotion walk, and gives S7 scheduling the tree-read interface it will need anyway. Candidate 2 is the strongest mud-preventer: the Symbol side-channel is hidden temporal coupling in the commit path, and the commit path is exactly where S7 arrives next. Candidates 4 and 3 follow before the first S5/S7 commit touches gantt-shell.ts or transaction.ts again.

Quick fixes worth batching with any of the above: mis-wired S4.5 harness button (spec), inert row-source keys and the reveal() fallback (standards), missing cause, duplicated doc block, rowResolutionInput name, and the five repeated catch (MutationCancelledError) blocks in harness/main.ts.
