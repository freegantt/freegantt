Two honest deepenings

A · Document the interface: “steps labels; density follows the floor only.”
B · Also write preferred density / fit:'preset' inside one Batch (contradicts D-S1.12-6).

Problem: the interface says “zoom in.” The implementation only swaps preset. Under default fit: 'pane', pxPerMs stays pane-fill until minTickWidthPx bites. canZoomIn can stay true while nothing visible happens.

Solution: do not silently rewrite Fit. Either state the contract on the public methods, or reopen D-S1.12-6 with a grill. Option B writes density as Anchored zoom, not as a second meaning of “zoom.”

Contradicts D-S1.12-6 if option B lands (“zoomIn/zoomOut step the preset; nothing else ever changes it”). Surface because HANDOFF already flags the UX as possibly a no-op. Do not rename Fit back to zoom (issue #84).
