Two honest deepenings

A · Document the interface: “steps labels; density follows the floor only.”
B · Also write preferred density / fit:'preset' inside one Batch (contradicts D-S1.12-6).

Problem: the interface says “zoom in.” The implementation only swaps preset. Under default fit: 'pane', pxPerMs stays pane-fill until minTickWidthPx bites. canZoomIn can stay true while nothing visible happens.
