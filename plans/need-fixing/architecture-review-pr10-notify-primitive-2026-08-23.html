Problem: CONTEXT.md's "Scale binding" entry explicitly reserves the word "subscribe" to warn readers off using it for binding — but the PR spends it on a different concept (change notification) that CONTEXT.md never names at all.

Solution: Name the notification concept in CONTEXT.md, and pick a verb for it with no textual echo of "bind" — onChange or whenResolved reads unambiguously next to bind/unbind.

  • Locality: one glossary entry answers "does subscribe mean bind?" everywhere, not per reader
  • Leverage: the interface's names stop needing tribal knowledge of an avoid-list to disambiguate
Same friction the codebase already paid down once for "chart" vs. GanttShell (issue #7) — a generic-sounding word covering two concepts in one interface.

Problem: plans/01 §8.2's D-A text frames the goal as every bound Gantt re-rendering "without anything external calling render() again" — but today the "subscribe → call render()" wiring is duplicated caller-side. The next thing that binds to a TimeScaleModel has to reimplement it.

Solution: Fold the reaction into bind()'s parameters (or the handle it returns), so the contract — "tell me what changed on your model, I'll call it" — is stated once by the interface instead of assumed by every caller.

  • Locality: "what happens on scale change" has one home instead of N call sites
  • Leverage: a future consumer gets correct behaviour for free instead of copying gantt-shell.ts's wiring
