FreeGantt — S3 direct manipulation

Drag a bar to move it. Drag an edge handle to resize. Click to select; Shift-click extends the selection. Arrow keys nudge a selection by one snap; Escape clears it and hands the arrows back to panning. Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes the last gesture. A drop before the dashed mobilization line is refused. Check “Hold drop” to keep the commit as a pending ghost until you uncheck it.

Check “Lock entry-15” and a Dataset plugin locks its three bars (S5.10). Drag entry-14: the locked bars ghost alongside it, because the plugin's extender wrote their dates too — and the drop is refused. entry-15's row draws three bars, and each one is a child Entry the row draws as a segment (childrenAsSegments). That is the case the cascade has to get right: it moves each bar rigidly through moveEntryTo, one dated Entry at a time.
