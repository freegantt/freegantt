About this page

The Gantt demo sandbox rebuild design, built on the public surface alone. Every colour is a published --fg-* token; every cell the design paints as something other than text is a columnRenderer; every bar colour is a barRenderer pointing --fg-bar-fill at a phase hue. Paper is the point of the exercise — the library ships Light and Dark, and Paper is a class this page defines over tokens the library never hears about.
