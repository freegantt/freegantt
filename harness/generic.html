Generic demo

A four-level fixture tree with grid columns, and one row that draws several bars — entry-16 draws its three children as segments (ADR 0026: a bar is a child Entry). The strip on the chart is the surface a consumer ships; everything under the torn rule drives the demo. Drag a bar up or down, or drag a row in the grid, to move it to another place. Lock tree turns both off. Lock a row (right-click → Lock): its bar and its grid row do not drag, and nothing drops into it.

Harness bench

Demo-only controls. None of this is library UI.

Rows and columns

Row source, filtering and column visibility. entry-16 (“Auth”) draws three bars on one row — its three child Entries, drawn as segments by childrenAsSegments.

Mutation

Every write goes through one transaction and one changeset. Double-click Name, Start or Budget in the grid to edit in place.

Activation (#434)

Click a bar or a grid row, or press Enter on one, to fire entryActivate. The readout above names the entry and the cause. Press Enter on a non-editable grid cell (Duration) to activate from there too — an editable cell opens its editor instead.

Convenience chords (#262)

Mod+Z/Mod+Shift+Z undo/redo are convenience chords: the toolbar's Undo/Redo buttons run the same command another way, so an app is free to turn the chord off and keep the keystroke for itself.

Vetoes and capabilities

A Mobilization date line marks a hard boundary — beforeEntryMove refuses a drop before it. Hold drop proves the async veto path.

Plugins and renderers

Each control is a plugin over the public contract alone. Mod+K clears the selection through a plugin-registered command.
