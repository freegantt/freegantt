Scheduling

The scheduling() plugin keeps Dependencies true. Drag a task later and its successors move. Drag it earlier and they follow. One undo restores every task. The loop at the bottom has no valid order, so the page lists its members. The constraint form sets one date rule on a task: a start floor, a finish ceiling or a must-start-on date. A ceiling that a Dependency breaks shows a finding. One working calendar shades the weekends and two holidays and drives the schedule: a successor never starts on a shaded day.
