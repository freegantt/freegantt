FreeGantt — bar label fit (#435)

Forty contiguous day-long bars, butted edge to edge, with barLabels: 'insideOrNone'. Each bar's label paints inside it when the day is wide enough for the text, and paints no label at all otherwise — never outside, where it would overlap the next day's bar. "Widen days" reassigns gantt.fit to a wider pixels-per-millisecond density live, the same knob a zoom gesture writes.
