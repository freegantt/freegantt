Dependencies

The entryDependencies() plugin stores the Dependencies between Entries. The Gantt draws one connector for each. This page shows all four types and one negative lag. Replace the whole set, and scroll, zoom and collapsed groups stay as they are. The Arrowheads box switches the arrowhead at the arrival end of each connector. Hover a bar and drag from one of its ports to a port on another bar to add a Dependency. Click a connector, or Tab to one, to select it. Arrow keys move to the next connector. Delete removes the Dependency, and one undo restores it.
